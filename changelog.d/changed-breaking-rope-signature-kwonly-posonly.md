- **Changed — BREAKING:** `change_signature` (and `argument_normalizer`, `argument_default_inliner`) now fails with `ToolInputError` (nothing written) for every operation on a function with keyword-only parameters (`*`) or positional-only parameters (`/`). rope cannot represent either separator: it previously emitted unparseable headers (`def f(a, c, *)`), lost parameters, failed with a generic error, or silently dropped the `/`. Migration: change such signatures by hand, or make the parameters ordinary positional ones for the call and restore the separator (ADR 0004).
