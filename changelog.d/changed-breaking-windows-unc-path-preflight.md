- **Changed — BREAKING:** On Windows, UNC (`\\server\share`, `//server/share`) and device-namespace (`\\?\UNC\...`, `\\?\Volume{...}`, `\\.\...`) paths are rejected with `[INVALID_INPUT]` before any filesystem access (they previously reached `resolve()` and could open a network connection), and a UNC `workspace_root` argument is refused at startup. The local `\\?\C:\...` spelling is still accepted. Migration: map the share to a drive letter. See ADR 0011.
